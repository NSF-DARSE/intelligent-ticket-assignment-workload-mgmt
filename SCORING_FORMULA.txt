TICKET ASSIGNMENT SCORING FORMULA

1. Core Recommendation Components

issue_type_skill_score
- Formula: same_issue_type_tickets / total_completed_tickets
- Purpose: measures technician experience with the same issue type

skill_experience_score
- Logic: combines employee skill alignment with issue-type experience
- Purpose: rewards technicians whose formal skills and historical experience both fit the ticket

bm25_text_expertise_score
- Logic: mean hybrid text-match support from similar historical tickets completed by that technician
- Purpose: rewards technicians with relevant lexical and semantic history on similar tickets

queue_group_skill_score
- Formula: same_queue_group_tickets / total_completed_tickets
- Purpose: measures familiarity with similar queue/category work

account_familiarity_score
- Formula: same_account_tickets / total_completed_tickets
- Purpose: gives credit for prior work on the same customer/account

workload_hours_score
- Formula: inverse-normalized from open_estimated_hours
- Purpose: favors technicians with lower current projected workload

workload_count_score
- Formula: inverse-normalized from open_ticket_count
- Purpose: favors technicians with fewer current open tickets

priority_balance_score
- Logic: lowers the score when a technician already holds many urgent tickets
- Purpose: prevents concentration of high-priority work on one person

resolution_efficiency_score
- Formula: inverse-normalized from avg_resolution_hours
- Purpose: rewards historically faster technicians

sla_pressure_score
- Logic: lowers the score when a technician already has many tickets with similar SLA pressure
- Purpose: balances SLA-sensitive workload

sla_urgency_fit_score
- Logic: combines urgency fit and current workload suitability
- Purpose: matches urgent tickets to technicians with the right capacity profile

complexity_fit_score
- Logic: compares ticket complexity with technician workload and capacity
- Purpose: avoids assigning harder tickets where current workload is already stretched

2. Recommendation Weights

Final Weighted Base Score =
0.20 * issue_type_skill_score
+ 0.18 * skill_experience_score
+ 0.12 * bm25_text_expertise_score
+ 0.08 * queue_group_skill_score
+ 0.04 * account_familiarity_score
+ 0.12 * workload_hours_score
+ 0.12 * workload_count_score
+ 0.08 * priority_balance_score
+ 0.05 * resolution_efficiency_score
+ 0.08 * sla_pressure_score
+ 0.08 * sla_urgency_fit_score
+ 0.10 * complexity_fit_score

3. Adjustment Metrics

capacity_penalty_score
- Logic: penalty when open_ticket_count or open_estimated_hours exceeds soft limits
- Purpose: prevents overloading one technician

distribution_penalty_score
- Logic: penalty when the technician is already above the current projected workload average
- Purpose: improves fair distribution across the team

new_technician_penalty_score
- Logic: penalty for risky tickets or exceeding onboarding limits
- Purpose: protects less-experienced technicians from unsafe assignments

low_risk_senior_penalty_score
- Logic: penalty for giving low-risk work to senior technicians
- Purpose: frees senior technicians for harder tickets

new_technician_bonus_score
- Logic: small bonus for safe low-risk assignments to newer technicians
- Purpose: improves onboarding and balanced exposure

low_risk_new_tech_bonus_score
- Logic: extra bonus for routing safe tickets to lower-history or less-used technicians
- Purpose: spreads workload and learning opportunities more fairly

4. Final Recommendation Score

Final Score =
Final Weighted Base Score
- capacity_penalty_score
- distribution_penalty_score
- new_technician_penalty_score
- low_risk_senior_penalty_score
+ new_technician_bonus_score
+ low_risk_new_tech_bonus_score

Then:

Final Score = max(0, Final Score)

5. Safety Rules

High-risk new-tech block
- Formula: Final Score = Final Score * 0.05
- Purpose: keeps high-risk tickets with experienced technicians

New-tech top-1 cap block
- Formula: Final Score = Final Score * 0.10
- Purpose: prevents one new technician from receiving too many top-1 assignments

6. Text Similarity Layer

The active text retrieval model is:
- BM25 = 40%
- MiniLM = 60%

This hybrid retrieval signal feeds the recommendation layer through historical text-expertise features rather than acting as the only decision factor.
