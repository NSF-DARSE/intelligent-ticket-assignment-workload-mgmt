TICKET ASSIGNMENT SCORING FORMULA

1. Base Metrics

issue_type_skill_score
- Formula: same_issue_type_tickets / total_completed_tickets
- Purpose: measures technician experience with this ticket type

queue_group_skill_score
- Formula: same_queue_group_tickets / total_completed_tickets
- Purpose: measures familiarity with similar queue/category work

account_familiarity_score
- Formula: same_account_tickets / total_completed_tickets
- Purpose: gives credit for prior work on the same customer/account

workload_hours_score
- Formula: inverse-normalized from open_estimated_hours
- Purpose: favors technicians with lower current estimated workload

workload_count_score
- Formula: inverse-normalized from open_ticket_count
- Purpose: favors technicians with fewer current open tickets

priority_balance_score
- Logic: lower score when technician already has many urgent tickets
- Purpose: prevents overload of high-priority work on one person

resolution_efficiency_score
- Formula: inverse-normalized from avg_resolution_hours
- Purpose: rewards historically faster technicians

sla_pressure_score
- Logic: lower score when technician already has many tickets of the same SLA pressure
- Purpose: balances SLA-sensitive workload

sla_urgency_fit_score
- Logic: combines urgency match and workload suitability
- Purpose: matches urgent tickets to appropriate technicians

complexity_fit_score
- Logic: based on ticket complexity vs technician workload
- Purpose: ensures difficult tickets are given where capacity exists

2. Base Recommendation Score

Base Score =
0.30 * issue_type_skill_score
+ 0.10 * queue_group_skill_score
+ 0.05 * account_familiarity_score
+ 0.15 * workload_hours_score
+ 0.15 * workload_count_score
+ 0.10 * priority_balance_score
+ 0.05 * resolution_efficiency_score
+ 0.10 * sla_pressure_score
+ 0.10 * sla_urgency_fit_score
+ 0.10 * complexity_fit_score

3. Adjustment Metrics

capacity_penalty_score
- Logic: penalty when open_ticket_count or open_estimated_hours exceeds soft limits
- Purpose: prevents overloading one technician

distribution_penalty_score
- Logic: penalty when technician is already above the current average projected workload in this run
- Purpose: improves fair distribution of recommendations

new_technician_penalty_score
- Logic: penalty for risky tickets or exceeding onboarding limits
- Purpose: prevents new technicians from receiving unsafe assignments

low_risk_senior_penalty_score
- Logic: penalty for giving low-risk work to senior technicians
- Purpose: frees senior technicians for harder tickets

new_technician_bonus_score
- Logic: small bonus for safe low-risk assignments to newer technicians
- Purpose: helps onboarding and balanced exposure

low_risk_new_tech_bonus_score
- Logic: extra bonus for distributing low-risk tickets to low-history / less-used technicians
- Purpose: improves workload spread

4. Final Recommendation Score

Final Score =
Base Score
- capacity_penalty_score
- distribution_penalty_score
- new_technician_penalty_score
- low_risk_senior_penalty_score
+ new_technician_bonus_score
+ low_risk_new_tech_bonus_score

Then:

Final Score = max(0, Final Score)

5. Safety Rules

High-risk new-tech block
- Formula: Final Score = Final Score * 0.05
- Purpose: keeps high-risk tickets with experienced technicians

New-tech top-1 cap block
- Formula: Final Score = Final Score * 0.10
- Purpose: prevents one new technician from receiving too many top-1 assignments

6. Business Interpretation

- High-risk, high-SLA, high-complexity tickets stay closer to experienced technicians.
- Low and medium-risk tickets can be redistributed more fairly.
- New technicians get controlled exposure through safe tickets.
- Workload balancing is handled both by current workload and projected recommendations during the same run.
